HPP Message Bot

A Slack app for daily High Performance Planning.

What it does

  • Mornings: asks your planning questions one at a time in a Slack direct message: what you’re looking forward to, what you’re thankful for, your compass, the things you need to get done (work, relations, physical, emotional/spiritual), and anything else on your mind.
  • When you’ve answered, it creates High Performance Planning YYYY-MM-DD in your own Google Drive, in a “High Performance Planning” folder.
  • During the day: check off tasks from the app’s Home tab or with /hpp update, and they turn gray in your doc.
  • Evenings: asks you to score six daily review statements from 1–10 and adds up your total.

Your data

The app uses Google’s drive.file permission, which only lets it open files it created itself. It can’t see anything else in your Drive. Your answers are kept only until your doc is created. You choose the times and days, and you can pause or leave at any time. Leaving deletes everything the app stores about you.

Privacy policy

Getting it

HPP Message Bot is available in the Slack workspace it’s installed in. Open HPP Message Bot under Apps in Slack’s sidebar and press Get started.
